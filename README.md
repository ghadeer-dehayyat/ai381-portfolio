## About Me

I'm an Artificial Intelligence student at **Jordan University of Science and Technology (JUST)**. I'm interested in understanding how AI systems are designed, evaluated, deployed, and improved—not only how models are trained.

This repository is my semester portfolio for **AI381: Machine Learning Operations (MLOps)**. It documents my labs, experiments, notes, and project milestones as I build practical skills step by step.

##  Goals for This Semester

- Understand the MLOps lifecycle, from experimentation to deployment.
- Practise reproducible workflows and version control with Git and GitHub.
- Explore tools for experiment tracking, model serving, and AI evaluation.
- Learn how to assess AI systems for reliability, performance, and safety.
- Turn course concepts into documented, practical work.

##  Lab Documentation Template

For each lab, I'll try to record:

- **Objective:** What problem or concept does the lab cover?
- **Approach:** What steps or tools did I use?
- **Results:** What happened, and what did I observe?
- **What I learned:** What became clearer?
- **Next steps:** What would I improve or explore further?

##  My Approach

I believe progress comes from consistent practice, asking good questions, learning from mistakes, and explaining what I build. My goal is to make this portfolio a clear record of both my technical work and my growth throughout the semester.
